Halkidiki is known for its beaches, but sun and sand are only half of it. Behind the coast are forested mountains, olive groves and quiet valleys; in front of it, some of the clearest water in the Aegean. For people who like to hike, paddle, cycle or ride, it is one of the best places in Greece to have a home. Here is where each activity is at its best, what kind of property suits an active life, and what to check before you buy.

## Why Halkidiki suits an active life

- **Mountains close to the sea.** In Sithonia and in northern Halkidiki you can walk a forest path in the morning and swim in the afternoon.
- **A long season.** The sea is warm enough to swim from May to October; spring and autumn are mild, the best time for walking and cycling.
- **Easy to reach.** Thessaloniki airport is about an hour from Kassandra, with flights all year, so a long weekend is realistic.
- **Space.** Around 550 km of coastline and large rural areas mean you are never far from somewhere quiet.

## Hiking and walking

**Sithonia** is the hiker's peninsula. Mount Itamos rises above Neos Marmaras, and the stone village of Parthenonas sits on its slopes with hiking trails around it and a view over the whole Toroneos gulf. The forest reaches down to the coves, so many walks end at the sea.

**Northern Halkidiki**, around Arnaia, is mountain country at 600 m altitude: forests, cooler summers and cobbled village streets. From there the sea at Ierissos and Olympiada is 35–40 minutes away.

**The Athos side** offers coastal walks around Olympiada and the ruins of ancient Stageira, Aristotle's birthplace. Mount Athos itself is a monastic community with its own entry rules for visitors.

## Kayaking, snorkelling and diving

The calm bays of Sithonia are ideal for paddling. **Vourvourou**, on the east coast, is a lagoon with small islands and the pine-covered Diaporos opposite: you can kayak or paddleboard between the islands and swim in shallow, clear water. The coves further south, towards Sarti and Sykia, are wilder and quieter. On the Athos side, **Olympiada** sits in a closed bay with calm sea. Kassandra's long sandy beaches are better for swimming and family water sports than for exploring by kayak.

## Cycling

Halkidiki suits both road and mountain bikes. The coastal roads give long rides with the sea in view; the inland roads and forest tracks of Sithonia and northern Halkidiki give climbs and quiet. Ride early in summer and enjoy spring and autumn, when the temperatures are best.

## Horse riding and the countryside

Away from the coast, Halkidiki is farming country: olive groves, vineyards, beekeeping and fields. It is good riding and walking land, and a plot here can give you space for animals and a garden that a seaside house cannot. Ask locally about riding in the area you are considering.

## Homes designed for an active life

What makes a home good for outdoor enthusiasts is mostly practical:

- **Storage.** A ground-floor room, garage or outbuilding for bikes, kayaks, paddleboards and diving gear, ideally with an outdoor shower and a place to rinse salt water off equipment.
- **Outdoor living.** A terrace or garden with shade, an outdoor kitchen or barbecue, and room to dry wetsuits and towels.
- **Location.** Walking distance to a trail or a quiet beach matters more than a sea view from the bedroom.
- **Parking.** Space for a car with a roof rack or a trailer.

Many houses in small complexes and maisonettes have shared gardens and parking; detached houses and village houses give more space. Our overview of [houses for sale in Halkidiki](/en/articles/all-news/homes-for-sale-in-halkidiki-greece) compares types and prices by area.

## Buying a plot near nature

For many outdoor lovers the ideal is a plot at the edge of a village or in the countryside, with a house designed around their life. That is possible, but plots need more care than houses:

- **Buildability.** Plots inside a settlement plan and fields outside it have very different building rights. We show the limits before the viewing, and your engineer confirms them.
- **Access and services.** Check the road, water and electricity. A beautiful plot without access costs a lot to make usable.
- **Protected areas.** Forests, coastlines and traditional settlements have extra rules; renovation rules apply to facades in places like Arnaia.
- **Legal check first.** Your lawyer checks title and encumbrances before any deposit.

Our guide to [plots for sale in Halkidiki](/en/articles/all-news/halkidiki-plots-for-sale) goes into detail, and the [land](/en/property-for-sale/land) and [plots](/en/property-for-sale/plots) pages show what is for sale.

## Year-round or seasonal?

An active life does not stop in October, but many beach villages do. Most beach villages in Sithonia and south Kassandra close from November to April. If you plan to live here, or to come for spring and autumn walks, choose a village with shops, a pharmacy and a doctor in winter: Nikiti, Neos Marmaras, Ierissos, Arnaia, Polygyros or the towns of Nea Propontida.

## Costs to plan for

Buying costs are the same for every type of property in Greece: about 6 % on top of the price, made up of 3.09 % transfer tax, notary and lawyer fees and the land registry. Every year you pay ENFIA, the property tax. Our [property tax guide with calculator](/en/articles/business-news/taxes-foreigners-properties-greece) gives the exact figures, and the [step-by-step buying guide](/en/articles/buyers/the-ultimate-guide-to-buying-property-in-halkidiki) explains the process.
