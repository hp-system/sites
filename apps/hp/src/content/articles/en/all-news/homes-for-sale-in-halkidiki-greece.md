## Types of homes

### Detached houses and houses in complexes

The classic Halkidiki holiday home: two levels, garden, often a shared or private pool, 200–800 m from a beach. Houses in small complexes cost less to maintain and are easier to let. From about €200,000; seafront and new builds from €400,000.

### Maisonettes

Two-level homes in a row or a small complex, usually 80–130 m², the most common choice for families from Thessaloniki and Sofia. From about €200,000.

### Apartments and studios

The most affordable way in: 35–75 m² near the beach in Kassandra, Sithonia or Nea Propontida, from €105,000. Easy to let, low running costs.

### Villas

Architect-designed houses with sea views, pool and large plots in Sani, Nikiti, Vourvourou, Afitos or Possidi. From about €600,000, many above the €400,000 Golden Visa threshold.

### Stone houses in villages

Traditional houses in Arnaia, Parthenonas, Afitos or Nikiti old town, often renovated with modern interiors; for buyers who want a village that lives all year.

## Buying a house in Halkidiki: the short version

1. Shortlist and viewings (we group them by peninsula, or show you by video).
2. Greek tax number (one day) and an independent lawyer for the legal check.
3. Pre-contract with a 10 % deposit; transfer tax 3.09 % paid before the deed.
4. Notary deed, bank transfer, land registry: usually 4–8 weeks in total.

## Frequently asked questions

How much does a house cost in Halkidiki?

Right now houses with a garden start from about €200,000, maisonettes from €200,000 and apartments from €100,000. Seafront villas in Sani, Nikiti or Vourvourou go from €600,000 to several million. All prices on our site are live.

Which area is best for a holiday home?

Kassandra for sandy beaches, nightlife and the strongest rental demand; Sithonia for pine forests, coves and quiet; Aristotelis for lower prices and space; Nea Propontida if you want to be 40 minutes from Thessaloniki all year.

Can foreigners buy a house in Halkidiki?

Yes, EU and non-EU citizens alike. Halkidiki is not a restricted border zone, so no permit is needed. You need a Greek tax number, issued in one day.

What are the extra costs when buying?
