**Sending us a request is the fastest way to see the homes in Halkidiki that really fit your budget.** Instead of scrolling through hundreds of listings that all look ideal, you tell us once what you need and we do the searching. Here is exactly what happens after you press "send", who contacts you, what you receive and how your details are handled.

The systems and website of Halkidiki Properties were rebuilt for one purpose: better customer service and a better experience for you as a buyer. Everything below is how that works in practice.

## Step 1: you send your request

You can send a request from any property page, from our [contact page](/en/contact) or, once you are registered, from your own account. A few details are enough to start:

- your budget, including whether it must also cover the purchase costs;
- the type of property (apartment, maisonette, house, villa, plot);
- the area, or simply "near the sea" or "a village that lives all year";
- the number of bedrooms and anything that is a must, such as a garden, a pool or parking;
- when you would like to buy and whether you will use the home yourself, rent it out, or both.

If you are not sure yet, that is fine. Many buyers start with "a house by the sea under a certain budget" and narrow it down with us.

## Step 2: a member of our team contacts you

As soon as we receive your request for a property in Halkidiki, a trained member of [our team](/en/about-us) contacts you directly by phone. If a call is not convenient, we communicate by SMS or e-mail instead.

The call takes a few minutes. By answering some easy questions about your preferences (budget, number of bedrooms and so on), you give us all the information we need about your criteria for buying a property. We also collect the contact details that make our collaboration faster, for example the best time and way to reach you.

## Step 3: you receive the matching properties

At the same time, a reply arrives in your e-mail with the properties that match your criteria. Every listing is photographed by our own team and its paperwork is checked before publication, so what you see is what exists.

The same e-mail contains the contact details of the agent who will work with you after our first call. **With that agent you go step by step through the whole process, until you buy the home you want.** You do not have to explain your needs again to someone new at each stage, and you always know whom to ask.

## Step 4: register to unlock everything

It is useful to [register on our website](/en/why-register) by opening any property link, if you have not done so already. Registration is free and unlocks all the fields of the website and all the information about each property:

- all photos and videos, and the exact location of each home;
- your favourites list, so you can compare the properties you like;
- the bell on each property: press it and you receive a notification if the price changes;
- your request itself, which you can fill in or change at any time.

**A useful tip:** while you browse [all properties](/en/properties), save your favourites and press the bell on the ones you are seriously interested in. When a seller lowers the price, you are among the first to know.

## Step 5: your request keeps working for you

Your request remains stored with us. The moment a new property is added that **meets your requirements, you receive a notification immediately**. Good homes in popular areas sell quickly, so this is often how buyers find the property they finally buy: not by searching every week, but by being told on the day it comes on the market.

If your plans change, for example a higher budget or a different peninsula, tell your agent or edit the request yourself, and the alerts follow the new criteria.

## Step 6: viewings and the next steps

When a few properties stand out, your agent arranges the viewings. We group them by peninsula so you see as much as possible in one day, or we show the properties to you by video if you cannot travel yet. Our guide to the [viewing trip day](/en/articles/buyers/viewing-trip-day) explains how to prepare.

After you choose, the purchase follows a fixed sequence: Greek tax number, an independent lawyer's check of the title and permits, pre-contract, transfer tax, notary deed and land registry. It usually takes four to eight weeks and costs about 6 % on top of the price. The full sequence is in our [step-by-step buying guide](/en/articles/buyers/the-ultimate-guide-to-buying-property-in-halkidiki), and the [property tax guide with calculator](/en/articles/business-news/taxes-foreigners-properties-greece#property-tax-calculator) gives the exact costs for any price.

## Why we say "your request is safe with us"

- **Your details are used only for your request.** We use them to contact you and to send you matching properties, as described in our [privacy policy](/en/privacy-policy). You can ask us to change or stop the alerts whenever you like.
- **You are not passed from hand to hand.** One agent, named in the first e-mail, follows your purchase from start to finish.
- **No pressure.** The request costs nothing and commits you to nothing. You decide if and when to view, and if and when to buy.
- **Local knowledge.** We are a local agency, working in Halkidiki since 2007, with our office in Nea Moudania and agents who live on the peninsulas and speak English, Bulgarian, Serbian, German and Greek.
