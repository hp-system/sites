Greece has always lived from tourism, and since the pandemic years the sector has carried even more of the economy, helped by government strategies to bring more visitors from abroad. Halkidiki is one of the places where this is most visible: an hour from Thessaloniki airport, three peninsulas of beaches, and a season that runs from May to October, with weekend visitors from Thessaloniki and the Balkans all year. If you are thinking of putting money into a property that earns from visitors, here is how we see the options in 2026, the rules that apply and what to check before you buy.

## Short answer

Tourism property in Halkidiki comes in three sizes: a holiday home you let for part of the year, a small building of rooms or apartments to let, and a hotel or other commercial tourism business. Buying costs about 6 % on top of the price, as anywhere in Greece. Short-term lets need a registration number (ΑΜΑ), and rental income is taxed from 15 %. Commercial property is outside the 15 % transfer tax announced for non-EU buyers from July 2027, and conversions of commercial buildings count for the Golden Visa from €250,000.

## Why Halkidiki for tourism investment

- **Access.** Thessaloniki airport is about an hour by car and open all year, with direct flights from Germany, the UK and the Balkans. Your guests do not depend on ferries or seasonal connections.
- **A long season.** May to October on the coast, plus families from Thessaloniki and visitors from the Balkans at weekends and holidays outside the summer.
- **A different market on each peninsula.** Kassandra has the sandy beaches, the resorts and the strongest rental demand. Sithonia is greener and quieter, with coves and villas. Aristotelis, towards Mount Athos, has the lowest prices per square metre. Nea Propontida lives all year as the commuter belt of Thessaloniki.
- **A wide pool of guests and buyers.** Greeks, Bulgarians, Serbians, Germans and Romanians come every year, which also gives you a wide resale market when you decide to sell.

## The three ways to invest

### 1. A holiday home that lets in summer

This is the most common choice among our buyers: a house, maisonette or apartment near a beach that you use yourself for part of the year and let to guests the rest of the time. Seasonal lets of well-located houses near the beach typically bring a gross return of about 4–6 % of the purchase price a year. It is also the easiest way in: [apartments](/en/property-for-sale/apartments) near the beach start from about €100,000 and houses with a garden from about €200,000. How owners balance their own holidays with bookings is explained in [Business and pleasure: buy and earn from your Halkidiki home](/en/articles/buyers/buy-and-create-passive-income-in-halkidiki-2022).

### 2. Rooms and apartments to let

A small building of studios or apartments in a beach village is a step up: more units, more income and more work. It suits buyers who will run it themselves or through a local manager. Look at the condition of the building, the parking, the distance to the beach and the guest reviews of the current operation, and ask the seller for the booking history before you agree on a price.

### 3. Hotels and commercial tourism property

Hotels, guesthouses and other commercial tourism buildings are a business purchase as much as a property purchase. Besides the building, you are buying a licence, a client base and a reputation. Your lawyer and engineer check that the building permits and the operating licence match what is actually built, and your accountant reads the figures of the last seasons. See what is on the market under [hotels and commercial property](/en/property-for-sale/hotels).

## The rules that matter in 2026

**Buying.** Transfer tax is 3.09 % of the price or the official value, whichever is higher. Notary and lawyer cost about 1 % each plus VAT, the land registry about 0.5 %. New builds sold under the VAT suspension until 31 December 2026 pay the same 3.09 % instead of 24 % VAT. The [cost calculator](/en/articles/business-news/taxes-foreigners-properties-greece#property-tax-calculator) gives you the figure for any price.

**Short-term letting.** A property let short-term through Airbnb, Booking or similar platforms needs a registration number, the ΑΜΑ. The guest pays the climate resilience fee for every night. Owners of up to two short-term properties pay no VAT on the rent.

**Rental income tax.** After a flat 5 % expense deduction: 15 % up to €12,000, 25 % from €12,001 to €24,000, 35 % from €24,001 to €35,000 and 45 % above. Example: €15,000 of rent leaves €14,250 taxable and €2,363 of tax.

**Selling.** Private individuals pay no capital gains tax until 31 December 2026. Sales that count as business activity, such as frequent sales or development, are taxed.

**Non-EU buyers.** A 15 % transfer tax on residential property for buyers from outside the EU and EEA has been announced for 1 July 2027. It is not law yet, and commercial property and land are excluded from it. The details are in our article on the [2027 transfer tax for non-EU buyers](/en/articles/buyers/greece-property-transfer-tax-non-eu-buyers-2027).

**Golden Visa.** In Halkidiki one residential property of at least 120 m² bought for €400,000 or more qualifies. Conversions of commercial buildings and listed buildings qualify from €250,000.

## In your own name or through a company?

Both are possible. Owning in your own name is simpler and currently has no capital gains tax. A company makes sense when you buy several properties or let professionally, for example a building of rooms or a hotel. Decide with your accountant before the pre-contract, because the choice is hard to change once the deed is signed.

## What to check before you buy

- The booking history and income of the last seasons, taken from the platforms, not from a summary.
- The ΑΜΑ or the operating licence, and whether it can continue under you.
- The building permits and the engineer’s certificate of no illegal construction.
- Access, parking, and what is open in the village from November to April.
- Who will clean, welcome guests and handle repairs when you are not there.
