[**Buying a home**](/en/properties) is one of the biggest financial decisions you will make, and it deserves careful thought. If you are looking for a home, or weighing up buying against renting, here are the benefits of owning property in Greece in 2026: residency, taxes, lifestyle, and the stability that comes with a home of your own. We also tell you plainly what it costs, so you can judge the benefits against the price.

## Short answer

Owning a home in Greece gives you a stable base, an asset in euro that tends to keep its value, and freedom to live as you like. A property of €400,000 or more in Halkidiki (at least 120 m²) can also give you and your family a five-year renewable residence permit. Taxes are moderate: about 6 % when you buy, a yearly ENFIA property tax, and no capital gains tax for private sellers until 31 December 2026.

## 1. Residency: the Golden Visa

Greece gives a five-year renewable residence permit to buyers of property above a threshold, for the buyer and the family, with no minimum stay. The thresholds in 2026:

| Where | Minimum investment |
| --- | --- |
| Halkidiki and most of Greece | €400,000, one property of at least 120 m² |
| Attica, Thessaloniki, Mykonos, Santorini, islands with more than 3,100 residents | €800,000 |
| Conversions of commercial buildings and listed buildings | €250,000 |

Halkidiki is outside the €800,000 zones, so the same permit costs half as much as in Athens or on Mykonos. The permit also gives access to travel in the Schengen area. Residency and taxation are separate questions, so check both with your lawyer before you sign. You can see [homes above the Golden Visa threshold](/en/property-for-sale/villas) among our villas and larger houses.

## 2. Taxes: what you pay and what you save

**When you buy:** transfer tax of 3.09 % of the price or the official value, whichever is higher, plus notary and lawyer (about 1 % each plus VAT) and land registry (about 0.5 %): about 6 % in total. New builds sold under the VAT suspension until 31 December 2026 pay the same 3.09 % instead of 24 % VAT.

**Every year:** ENFIA, the unified property tax, charged per square metre by zone. In Halkidiki most holiday homes fall between €2.5 and €10 per m²; a 100 m² house in a coastal village is typically €400–700 a year. Discounts in 2026: 20 % for homes insured against earthquake, fire and flood for the full year, and 50 % for a primary residence in a village of up to 1,500 inhabitants (value up to €400,000). A small municipal fee (TAP) is charged through the electricity bill.

**If you let it:** rental income is taxed separately after a flat 5 % deduction, at 15 % up to €12,000 and higher above that.

**When you sell:** private individuals pay no capital gains tax until 31 December 2026.

**For non-EU buyers:** a 15 % transfer tax on residential property has been announced for 1 July 2027; it is not law yet. Details in our article on the [2027 transfer tax for non-EU buyers](/en/articles/buyers/greece-property-transfer-tax-non-eu-buyers-2027), and every rate in the [property tax guide](/en/articles/business-news/taxes-foreigners-properties-greece).

## 3. Lifestyle

Greece is a country you live outdoors: sea, mountains, villages with a square and a taverna, and a Mediterranean climate with hot summers and mild winters. In Halkidiki you have beaches and pine forests an hour from Thessaloniki, an international airport and a city with hospitals, universities and culture. Towns such as Nea Moudania, Kassandria, Nikiti and Polygyros live all year, so a holiday home can become a permanent one. Many of our buyers let the house for ten summers and move in when they retire.

## 4. Stability and security

Owning a home gives you stability, especially if you plan to live in Greece long-term or return every year. You no longer depend on a landlord, on rent rises or on the end of a lease. The home is yours, registered at the land registry, and you decide when and whether to move.

## 5. A long-term investment

A home is a long-term investment. Over the years you build up an asset you can use, let, sell or pass on to your children. A well-located property can earn rent in the meantime: seasonal lets of houses near the beach in Halkidiki typically bring a gross return of about 4–6 % of the purchase price a year. More in [why invest in Greek real estate](/en/articles/buyers/investing-in-real-estate).

## 6. Protection against inflation

Real estate is widely seen as a hedge against inflation: when prices rise, the value of a well-located home tends to rise with them, while cash savings lose value. A property in Greece is also priced in euro, whatever happens to your home currency. We do not promise appreciation; before you buy, we show you the sales history of the area.

## 7. Freedom and control

Finally, owning a home gives you freedom over your living space. You can decorate, renovate and use the garden as you like, without asking a landlord. You can make it truly your own.

## What owning costs

To weigh the benefits honestly, add up the costs: about 6 % when you buy, ENFIA every year, insurance, upkeep, and an accountant for your annual Greek tax return (about €150–250). A pool adds the luxury living tax. None of these is large on its own, but plan for them before you commit.
