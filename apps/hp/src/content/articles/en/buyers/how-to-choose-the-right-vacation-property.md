Choosing a holiday home is less about finding the “perfect” house and more about answering a few questions honestly: what kind of holidays you like, who comes with you, how often you will be there and whether you will let it. Answer them before you start scrolling through listings, and the shortlist almost writes itself. Here are the questions we go through with every buyer looking for a holiday property in Halkidiki.

## Short answer

Decide first between a **quiet** and a **lively** area, then between a **summer village** and a **town that lives all year**, and only then between an **apartment** (cheaper, easier to let, close to shops) and a **maisonette or house** (garden, space, views). Halkidiki gives you all the combinations within an hour of Thessaloniki airport.

## 1. What kind of holiday do you want?

Do you enjoy beaches with few people and pine forest down to the sand, or something more cosmopolitan, with beach bars, tavernas and villages with nightlife? The answer narrows the map more than anything else.

The answer is not as simple as “Kassandra or Sithonia”. Many people think of these two peninsulas as having the best beaches in Halkidiki, and they do, but each has both quiet and busy corners. And Athos, the third peninsula, is a category of its own, with religious tourism around the Holy Mountain and a much smaller summer crowd than most other places.

**The quieter areas** are most of the villages of Sithonia, except Nikiti, Neos Marmaras and Kalyves, and in Kassandra the areas of Sani, Mola Kalyva, Paliouri and Kriopigi.

**The livelier areas** are the resort villages of Kassandra and the larger centres of Sithonia, where you find beach bars, restaurants and nightlife in July and August.

## 2. Which peninsula suits you?

- **[Kassandra](/en/properties/kassandra)**, the first peninsula, about 70 km from the airport: sandy beaches, resorts (Sani, Afitos, Hanioti, Pefkochori), year-round villages and the strongest rental demand.
- **[Sithonia](/en/properties/sithonia)**, the middle peninsula: pine forests to the sea, coves, Nikiti, Neos Marmaras, Vourvourou and Sarti. Quieter, with villas and sea views.
- **[Aristotelis](/en/properties/aristotelis)**, eastern Halkidiki towards Mount Athos: Ierissos, Ouranoupoli, Olympiada. The lowest prices per square metre and large plots.
- **[Nea Propontida](/en/properties/nea-propontida)**, 40–60 km from Thessaloniki: Nea Moudania, Nea Kallikratia, Nea Potidea. Schools, hospital and shops all year; the best choice if the holiday home may become a permanent one.

## 3. Summer village or a town that lives all year?

Many beach villages in Sithonia and south Kassandra close from November to April. That is perfect for a summer house and a problem if you want to come at Christmas or Easter. Nea Moudania, Nea Kallikratia, Kassandria, Nikiti, Neos Marmaras, Ierissos, Arnaia and Polygyros have schools, shops and doctors in winter. If you plan to stay outside the summer, visit in November too, or ask us for a video. Our guide to [villages worth knowing before you buy](/en/articles/all-news/hidden-gems-lesser-known-villages-in-halkidiki-worth-exploring) describes what each one is like to live in.

## 4. Where do your friends have a home?

It helps to have friends in the area. You feel more secure with your choice, someone can check on the house when you are away, and if you want to spend summer evenings together you will not have to drive many kilometres.

## 5. Apartment or maisonette?

### Holiday apartments

The advantage of a holiday [apartment](/en/property-for-sale/apartments) is economy: a lower price, lower running costs and easy letting. From about €100,000 near the beach. Most apartments are built inside the villages, so you have easy holidays next to supermarkets, bakeries and tavernas, without needing the car every day.

### Maisonettes

A [maisonette](/en/property-for-sale/maisonettes), usually two levels of 80–130 m² in a row or a small complex, is the most common choice for families from Thessaloniki and Sofia, from about €200,000. It gives you the most possibilities:

- You can invite people to eat in your own garden.
- Children can play outside safely.
- Barbecue in the open air, not on a balcony.
- Ideal if you have pets.
- Space for two families or more.
- Generous rooms and storage.
- Often outside the village, with greenery and views: the best relaxation after a day at the beach.

### Houses and villas

Detached [houses](/en/property-for-sale/houses) with a garden, often with a shared or private pool, start from about €200,000; architect-designed [villas](/en/property-for-sale/villas) with sea views from about €600,000. They give you privacy, at the cost of more upkeep.

## 6. Will you let it?

If letting is part of the plan, choose with your guests in mind: walking distance to a beach, parking, air conditioning and outdoor space matter more to them than an extra bedroom. Kassandra has the strongest rental demand. Short-term lets need a registration number (ΑΜΑ), and rental income is taxed from 15 %. How owners combine their own holidays with letting is in [buy and earn from your Halkidiki home](/en/articles/buyers/buy-and-create-passive-income-in-halkidiki-2022).

## 7. What is your full budget?

Add about 6 % to the price for taxes and fees (transfer tax 3.09 %, notary, lawyer, land registry), and keep a reserve for furniture and small works. Our [cost calculator](/en/articles/business-news/taxes-foreigners-properties-greece#property-tax-calculator) gives the exact figure for any price.

## Before the viewing trip

Make a short list of what is mandatory and what you can compromise on, and keep it in mind during viewings. Ask to see each property at two different hours of the day, and spend an evening in the village you like before you decide.
