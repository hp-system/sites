## 1. How is the real estate market in Halkidiki doing?

The real estate market in Halkidiki is currently booming. With growing tourism and the COVID-19 breakout, more and more people are turning to the countryside and coastal areas. The increase in demand, combined with a limited supply of quality homes, has caused a hike in property prices. However, experts claim that this trend is predicted to continue for a while. Hence, it's an excellent time to invest in a property in Halkidiki and expect a good return on investment.

## 2. Do I need to hire a real estate agent?

Hiring a local real estate agent is highly recommended, especially if you are unfamiliar with the local real estate market, regulations, and tax laws. A professional real estate agent will provide you with valuable insights, guide you through the entire buying process, and ensure you get the best deal possible. Moreover, they can also introduce you to local contractors, help with legal work and property inspections.

## 4. What are the legal requirements for buying property in Greece?

Foreign buyers in Greece need to be aware of the legal requirements for buying property. International buyers are subject to the same real estate regulations as Greek citizens and require permission from the Council of State. Additionally, legal representation, a legal report, and a notary public's presence solemnize the transaction. These processes could take a few months to complete, so keep the timeline in mind when planning your purchase.

## 5. How much should I budget for buying a property in Halkidiki?

Determining your budget will depend on your long-term goals, financial situation, and future plans for the property. However, it's always a good idea to have a buffer as unforeseen costs such as taxes, property insurance, maintenance costs, and utilities can quickly add up.

## 6. How is the weather in Halkidiki?

Halkidiki has a Mediterranean climate, which means that it experiences hot, dry summers from May to September and mild, rainy winters between November and February .

## 7. What amenities are available in Halkidiki?

Halkidiki offers plenty of amenities including supermarkets, banks, post offices, and hospitals. The larger towns have plenty of restaurants, bars, and shops, while the beaches offer ample opportunities for water sports and other leisure activities. The beaches in Halkidiki are some of the best in Greece and provide many opportunities to soak in the sun, take in the scenery or go for a swim.
