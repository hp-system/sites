Viewing trip day is the day you visit, together with your agent, the properties you have chosen. It is the most important step before buying a property in Halkidiki: it decides which home you buy, and it gives you a real picture of the area where this life investment will take place. A well-prepared trip ends with a clear shortlist; a rushed one ends with a confused evening and no answers. Here is how we prepare it with you, what the day looks like, and what happens afterwards.

## Before the trip: narrow the search to your real needs

Searching online among [hundreds of houses and plots](/en/properties) can make the viewing trip harder, not easier. When you start looking for a summer property, the options are endless and most of them look ideal. The focus should stay on what you really need.

If, for example, you need an apartment and can pay up to €100,000, there is no point in looking at maisonettes or detached houses at €300,000. What usually happens otherwise is that we collect a large number of properties, view them in a rush, and by the end of the day you are confused and leave without real results.

So the step that comes before a viewing trip day is selecting properties from photos and your interests. Together with your agent, make a list of your favourite choices. Registered members see all photos, videos and exact locations, which makes this shortlist far more reliable; you can also ask your agent for a video call of any home before you decide it is worth the trip.

## Practical preparation

- **Tell your agent your arrival date 7–10 days in advance.** This is what lets us arrange your visits properly and confirm every property with its owner.
- **Decide what is mandatory and where you can compromise.** Keep that list in mind so that you are not confused during the search. Sea view, distance to the beach, a garden, a village that lives all year: you rarely get everything, so know your priorities.
- **Expect the details in advance.** Several days before the trip you receive all the important information: the meeting point, the time and the properties you will visit.
- **Bring your personal documents.** At the end of the viewing trip you sign a brokerage agreement, so have your passport or identity card with you.
- **Bring a pen, your questions and all your senses.** Buying a property is a serious decision. Our agent gives you a booklet with the details of every property you visit, so you do not have to stress about remembering them.
- **Know your total budget.** Buying in Greece costs about 6 % on top of the price in taxes and fees; our [cost calculator](/en/articles/business-news/taxes-foreigners-properties-greece#property-tax-calculator) gives the exact figure, so you view homes you can really afford.

## The day itself

You meet your agent, usually in the morning so that you are fresh and have the whole day ahead of you. Your agent explains the schedule and the programme, and then you start visiting the properties you have liked.

**Plan on eight to ten homes in a day.** That leaves more than 30 minutes for each one, enough to look carefully and reach a realistic conclusion on whether it matches your needs. We group the viewings by peninsula, so little of the day is lost on the road. If a property is important to you, ask to see it at two different hours of the day, and in the state in which it will be delivered.

After seeing them all, if you are lucky, you will have a shortlist of two or three. Then comes the part many buyers skip: **investigate the neighbourhood and the amenities nearby.** Ideally, arrange a dinner, an evening walk or even a day trip in the areas where the properties you liked are located. Let us not forget that literally everything in a house can change except its location.

If you plan to live in the home rather than use it in summer, remember that many beach villages close from November to April. Visit, or ask us for video, out of season as well; our guide to [villages that live all year](/en/articles/all-news/hidden-gems-lesser-known-villages-in-halkidiki-worth-exploring) helps here.

## The 10 questions clients ask most during the viewing

1. How is the neighbourhood?
2. What does the surrounding environment look like?
3. Is the furniture included in the price?
4. Does anybody live in the house at the moment?
5. What is the annual property tax? (The yearly ENFIA tax depends on the size and location; a 100 m² house in a coastal village is typically €400–700 a year.)
6. What are the extra costs for the maintenance of the building or complex?
7. What is the beach nearby like?
8. What condition are the PVC window frames in?
9. Is there parking and a storage room?
10. How does the heating work in winter?

More answers are in our [frequently asked questions](/en/faq).

All of these questions matter on the technical side, but at the end of the day what counts is **how you feel** about the home. Try to imagine yourself in that house, in that environment and place. Do you see yourself there? If the answer is yes, it is a strong candidate. If not, keep searching for the right property. Most minor details can be solved.

## At the end of the day

Feel free to have an open discussion with your agent: ask questions and be direct about your thoughts and doubts. Buying a property by the sea is an important moment, and our job is to make it a good experience. With years of local experience behind us, we will have answers or solutions to most of your concerns, and you can always expect a professional attitude and honest advice.

## After the viewing trip

Take as long as you need to think and to analyse the pros and cons of every property you have visited. That is how you get a clear picture of what you really want and need. Then check whether any of them meets your initial requirements.

Once you choose, the next steps are fixed: Greek tax number, an independent lawyer's check of title and permits, pre-contract with a deposit, transfer tax, notary deed and land registry, usually four to eight weeks in total. Our [step-by-step buying guide](/en/articles/buyers/the-ultimate-guide-to-buying-property-in-halkidiki) explains each one.

Having found the right house in the best location for you, you can proceed with the purchase and enjoy your choice for the rest of your life. After that step, you know what is next: you go for it, or you [keep searching](/en/properties).
