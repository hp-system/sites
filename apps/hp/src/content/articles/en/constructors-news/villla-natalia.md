## By Halkidiki Properties Constructions


### [Villa Natalia](https://villanatalia.eu/), the ultimate gem of North Greece,

a private villa that combines the utmost luxury with a wide variety of amenities, providing a unique, unprecedented experience! A great sea view magically binds with the two swimming pools, creating the best atmosphere for romantic evenings and enchanting nights.

 The main house offers six suites, each with its own bathroom, fully equipped with everything that the guest might need during their luxurious stay. A high ceiling living area on the ground floor is the meeting point where the guests can relax and enjoy with their company around the fireplace or eat around the dining table. The house includes a play house, a library and a gym, as well as a traditional sauna. Two kitchens come to complete the picture, one on the inside of the house and another one on the patio, with barbeque and another bamboo dining table for six.

Two more private suites stand individually on the outside of the house, both with their own kitchen and bathrooms. These suites lead directly to the small pool, with a sitting area for four people and a magnificent view through the glass wall, where the pool surface comes in line with the sea level and the passing fishing boats project through the water.

The outside of the house consists of a garden that covers an area of 2,5 acres, two swimming pools and a private beach. There is a playground for the kids, a tennis court, a mini-football court, a portable basketball goal and a beach volley court. There is also a vegetable garden with several organic vegetables, where the guests can select their own fresh tomatoes, cucumbers, peppers etc. A plane tree with a natural spring underneath is another great sitting area, offering natural shadow under the thick branches  of the tree.

The infinity, olympic-size pool is heated with adjustable temperature and is surrounded by poofs, sunbeds and several sitting areas, as well as a number of luxurious wooden lounge beds, that offer great options for moments of total relaxation.

The second, smaller pool is also heated and is ideal for children, since it is pretty shallow and gives peace of mind to the parents, who can let the kids play freely in the water. This pool offers a unique effect of a glass wall, that creates a mesmerising effect in combination with the crystal blue waters of the sea.

The villa offers many technological breakthroughs, too. It is a smart house, that can easily be functioned remotely from any smart phone or tablet. House temperature, humidity, underfloor heating/cooling, air conditioning, lighting, locking/unlocking, pool temperature and air quality are only a few of the things that the visitor can control from their phone. Each room has a separate wall-mount i-pad that controls the temperature, humidity and lights individually. There are geothermal pumps that gather heat from the soil and transfer it to the pool, as needed, as well as solar panels on the roof, technologies that reduce significantly the energy demands of the villa. The pools are both automatically kept clean and are naturally clorinationed through the method of alas electrolysis, while the big one is also cleaned with ozon, a method that is proven to keep the water crystal clean. There is an biological wastewater treatment that covers efficiently the needs of the house, as well as large water tanks.

In overall, due to the combination of all the functional and technological features, it is absolutely a one-of-a-kind villa, the best property in the Municipality of Halkidiki and probably the best in all North Greece.
