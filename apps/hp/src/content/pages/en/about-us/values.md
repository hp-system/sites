## Our company's core values

As our team grows, it becomes increasingly important to identify and describe the core values that guide what we do. Our core values develop and define our culture, our brand, our business strategies and who we are as professionals in today's corporate world. Our goal is to continually live our lives on a daily basis with these values and encourage both our teammates and our clients to be accountable to us for these values.

In addition to our core values, our mission statement more clearly defines our true purpose as an organization and further enhances our team culture. We are united in the consistent observance of our core values and the execution of our professions in direct alignment with our mission statement. We are committed to providing world-class services and top know-how to our customers. We are passionate about providing extra value that others simply will not. The fanatical integrity and consistently impressive results will be the signature of our service. Our collaborative spirit and our desire to grow will help us achieve the goals we set. We will make the families we serve feel special, creating ambitious fans and helping them really win. We will succeed because we will not let our customers fail.

- **We love** our country and its beauties, but we love the Greek way of life more. Living in Greece changes you as a person.
- We have **a deep knowledge** of the object, the place and the life of the people.
- **We believe in people's passion** for creation and uniqueness.
- The needs and **interests of our customers** are at the heart of everything we do.
- Human **relationships** mean everything to us.
- **We connect** people with their homes and their communities.
- **We offer only quality services** and aim **to exceed expectations** in what we do.
- We behave with **a sense of responsibility** to the **highest moral standards**, showing **honesty** and **justice** in every decision and action.
- **We never forget** that we **owe** everything to the efforts of each member of the **team**, our **families** and our **customers.**
- We know that **we are selling real estate properties** and not a temporary good and we help our customers to always make the right choice.
- We are not a classic Real Estate company with independent partners competing with each other. We are **a single group of people who work together** for the ultimate satisfaction of our customers.
- We select **partners** with the focus of our attention focused on their **ethos.**
- We know very well the new **technologies** and we are working feverishly on **innovations** that will change the Real Estate market.
- **We embrace** the **new ideas** and the **bold thinking.**

### Our people

They travel thousands of miles regardless of the weather, selling and building houses, building relationships, solving problems, negotiating, going underground, jumping fences, photographing, get wet, discovering neighborhoods. They are obsessed with the prices, the market values and the new properties. They make sure and act to ensure that our customers get the ideal home for them. They are always available, but above all they love their job.

They are the people who make the companies and not the other way around.
