## We are looking for

Do you speak Bulgarian? A new position is opening a new door in your life!

We are currently looking for an enthusiastic & communicative Customer Support Agent. Ready to join your next adventure?

You will handle the first communication with our Bulgarian clients and insert their data and request criteria in our company's informatics systems.

## Requirements
- Native-level or C2 level in Bulgarian
- At least B2 level in English
- Passionate about new technologies
- High School Diploma
- Great communication skills
- Prior customer support experience would be an advantage

## Benefits
- Full-time
- Competitive salary – insurance coverage

To apply, send your CV by e-mail to [gtsigaras@halkidikiproperties.gr](mailto:gtsigaras@halkidikiproperties.gr) with "Bulgarian speaker" in the subject line, or send us the form below and we will reply.
