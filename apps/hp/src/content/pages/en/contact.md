We are ready to guide you in any process, whether you are interested in buying or selling a property, or are looking for a real estate adviser in Halkidiki.

Prefer to see properties from where you are? [Book an online appointment](/en/book-an-online-appointment) and view them live by video call.

## How to reach us

Call our office on the phone number above during opening hours, Monday to Friday from 09:30 to 19:00 and Saturday from 10:00 to 14:00, or send us an e-mail at any time. If you prefer a message, write to us on WhatsApp or Viber: an adviser answers in your language — we speak Greek, English, German, Russian, Bulgarian and Serbian.

## Visit our office

Our office is in Neo Rysio, next to Thessaloniki airport "Macedonia", on the way to the Halkidiki peninsulas. Every service around a property is in one place: sales advisers, our legal and accounting support, and the engineering and architecture department. Use the map link above to get directions, and tell us when you plan to come so that the right adviser is there for you.

## What to send us

The more we know, the faster we can help:

- **Buying:** the area you like ([Kassandra](/en/property-for-sale/kassandra), [Sithonia](/en/property-for-sale/sithonia), [Aristotelis](/en/property-for-sale/aristotelis), [Nea Propontida](/en/property-for-sale/nea-propontida)), the type of property, your budget and when you would like to buy. You can also browse [all properties for sale](/en/properties) first.
- **Selling:** the location of your property, its size and a few photos. Read how we [sell your property](/en/sell) and [advertise it worldwide](/en/sell/advertise-your-property-worldwide).
- **Building or renovating:** what you have in mind and where. See our [construction services](/en/services/construction-services).

An adviser replies with a shortlist or the next steps, and can arrange a viewing in person or by video call.
