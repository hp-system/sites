## How to read these prices

These are **asking prices** of the properties we list today, not final sale prices. For homes the price per square metre is the price divided by the floor area; for land it is the price divided by the plot size. The **median** is the middle value — half of the listings ask more, half ask less — and the **middle half** shows the range of the central 50 % of listings, which is a fairer picture than the cheapest or the most expensive property.

Prices are computed automatically from our current listings and update continuously, so they follow the market as properties are added or sold. A municipality or a village appears only when there are enough listings for a reliable figure.

## What moves the price in Halkidiki

- **Distance to the sea.** Seafront homes and homes a short walk from the beach ask clearly more per m² than homes inland.
- **Peninsula.** Kassandra and Sithonia, with the big resorts and the longest season, ask more than Nea Propontida, which is closer to Thessaloniki and lives all year.
- **Type and condition.** New builds and renovated houses in small complexes with a pool ask more per m² than older apartments.

For a specific property, an adviser can give you the asking prices of comparable homes nearby. See the [properties for sale in Halkidiki](/en/properties) or [contact an adviser](/en/contact).
