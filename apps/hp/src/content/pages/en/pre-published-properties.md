## What "pre-published" means

A pre-published property is one we are preparing for the market: the owner has decided to sell, and we are checking the paperwork, taking our own photos and setting the price. Until the listing is ready, it is not on the portals or in our public search.

Registered members see these properties first, with the municipality, the type, the size and the asking price, and can mark the ones that interest them. When a property you marked becomes available, we tell you before anyone else.

## Who it is for

- **Buyers with a clear brief** — a type, an area and a budget — who want to see suitable homes, land or hotels before they appear on the open market.
- **Investors** looking for land, plots and professional properties across the four municipalities of Halkidiki: [Kassandra](/en/property-for-sale/kassandra), [Sithonia](/en/property-for-sale/sithonia), [Aristotelis](/en/property-for-sale/aristotelis) and [Nea Propontida](/en/property-for-sale/nea-propontida).
- **Buyers abroad**, who can follow the market from their screen and book an [online viewing](/en/book-an-online-appointment) the moment a property is ready.

## How it works

1. **Register for free.** Membership costs nothing and asks only for your name, e-mail and phone. Read [why register](/en/why-register).
2. **Tell us your criteria.** Type, area, budget and size — or simply send the form below.
3. **Get informed first.** You receive timely information from our advisers as soon as a matching property becomes available, before it is published.
