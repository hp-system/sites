Every listing has our own photos, checked paperwork and a real price, and the prices on this page update automatically from our listings.

## How to buy property in Greece – 6 steps

1. **Shortlist and viewings.** We shortlist by your criteria and arrange viewings over 1–2 days, or [online](/en/book-an-online-appointment).
2. **Tax number (ΑΦΜ).** Issued in one day at the tax office in Polygyros with your passport; we accompany you. No special permit is needed for foreigners in Halkidiki.
3. **Lawyer and due diligence.** An independent lawyer checks title, encumbrances and building permits — typically 1 % + VAT.
4. **Pre-contract and deposit.** Usually a 10 % deposit. The price is fixed and the property comes off the market.
5. **Notary and payment.** The final deed is signed before a notary; payment is by bank transfer with proof of funds.
6. **Land registry.** After registration the property is yours. Electricity, water and ΕΝΦΙΑ are transferred to your name.

## Taxes and costs when buying (2026)

- **Transfer tax:** 3.09 % of the price (resales, and new builds while VAT is suspended until 31 December 2026).
- **Notary** about 1 % + VAT, **lawyer** about 1 % + VAT, **land registry** about 0.5 %, **agency** 2 % + VAT.
- **In total:** about 6 % on top of the price before the agency commission, about 8–9 % with it.
- **Annual ΕΝΦΙΑ:** a 100 m² house by the sea usually pays €400–700 per year.
- **Non-EU buyers:** Greece announced on 6 September 2026 a 15 % transfer tax from 1 July 2027. It is not yet law; 3.09 % applies until then.
