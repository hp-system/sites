## We register the client in your name and work for you

- We start communication with the customer.
- We enter their demand or their property and immediately send offers.
- We find the ideal property for your client, or send their property to suitable clients.
- We organise a viewing trip to every property in Halkidiki.

## Why work with Halkidiki Properties

- **Instant, automatic updates** for your customers via Viber, WhatsApp, e-mail and SMS.
- **Properties all over Halkidiki**, with new listings added every day.
- **A large database of potential buyers** built since 2007.
- **Constant communication** between our representatives and your customers.

## What you earn

- **Buyer:** on contract day, you simply collect your fee from your client's side.
- **Owner:** on contract day, you simply collect 1 % of the brokerage fee.

The partnership follows our [collaboration order](/en/collaboration-order). Send the form below and an adviser will contact you to register your first client.
