## Advertise your property

**The largest portals in the world are our partners**.

[Halkidiki Properties](/en) has developed automated real estate synchronisation bridges with the largest advertising portals in the world.

When a property enters our database and after first gathering the necessary property sales index, it automatically synchronises with various websites around the world.

Indicatively, we mention the following collaborators who, with their collaborating subpages, list your property on many portals in many languages.

**Greece:** Spitogatos, Tospitimou, Spiti24, Xe

**Germany:** Immowelt, Immobilienscout24, Ferimmo

**Russia:** Tranio, Prian

**Bulgaria:** Luximmo

**Serbia:** Indomia, Srbija nekretnine

**England:** Rightmove

**China:** Juwai

**Globally:** Domaza, Green Acres
