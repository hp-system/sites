## Get real-time visits

Within just 24 hours of the publication of your property receive **real visits** from **real clients.**

From the very first day of the publication of your property on the **Halkidiki Properties** page, it becomes known, and we already receive visits and requests from potential buyers.

After you choose the way and the frequency that you want to be informed, our company sends you special reports which keep you informed about the potential buyers of your property.

We are also developing a unique **Owner’s Panel** system through which you will be able to track the sale of your property, as well as detailed statistics.

## Reports instead of guesswork

Selling is easier when you know what buyers think. After your property is published you receive reports on its visits and on the requests of potential buyers, at the frequency you choose. They show whether the price and the presentation work, and help us decide together on the next steps — new photos, a better description or a price review.

## What you can do to get more visits

- Give us good photos, or let us arrange [professional photography and videos](/en/services/seller-services).
- Have the documents ready: title deed, topographic plan and energy certificate (PEA).
- Keep the property easy to visit, or allow [online viewings by video call](/en/book-an-online-appointment) for buyers abroad.

Buyers who live abroad often decide after a video viewing, so a property that is ready to be shown online sells faster. Ready to start? [Add your property](/en/add-your-property) or [talk to an adviser](/en/contact).
