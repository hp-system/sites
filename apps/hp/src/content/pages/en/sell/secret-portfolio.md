Would you like not to publish your property **publicly,** but only to **certified buyers?**

If the answer is yes, then our **Exclusive Secret Portfolio** service is ideal for you.

Our company gathers and certifies buyers through the **Register as Premium, Business + and VIP Membership.**

Interested users, if they successfully pass the identification process, gain special access to the **Exclusive Secret Portfolio** of Halkidiki Properties and consequently to the properties that are contained in it.

So your property, even though it is not openly advertised, continues to be displayed exclusively to the most closely connected users, who are also interested in very specific and special properties.

The **Exclusive Secret Portfolio** is a product that is addressed mainly to luxury properties, hotels, but also smaller special properties whose owners do not want their public display.

If you are interested in assigning your property through the **Exclusive Secret Portfolio** product, contact us with the form below or give us a [call](/en/contact).
