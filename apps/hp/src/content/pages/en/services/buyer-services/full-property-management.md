Are you an owner of a property in Halkidiki, Greece, and are you looking for a reliable partner to manage it for you? Look no further than Halkidiki Properties.

We offer comprehensive property management services, designed to take the hassle and stress out of owning a property. Our team of experienced professionals will take care of everything, from finding tenants to maintaining the property and everything in between.

## What our full property management includes

- **Tenant Screening and Management:** We'll help you find and screen tenants to ensure that they are reliable and trustworthy. We'll also manage the lease agreement and handle all communication with tenants.
- **Rent Collection and Financial Management:** We'll collect rent on your behalf and handle all financial aspects of the property, including paying bills and taxes.
- **Property Maintenance and Repairs:** We'll take care of all necessary maintenance and repairs, ensuring that your property is always in top condition.
- **Property Inspections:** We'll conduct regular inspections to ensure that your property is well-maintained and any issues are identified and addressed promptly.
- **Marketing and Advertising:** We'll handle all aspects of marketing and advertising your property, from creating listings to showing the property to potential tenants.

## Why owners trust us

At [Halkidiki Properties](/en), we pride ourselves on providing exceptional customer service and taking a proactive approach to property management. We'll work closely with you to understand your needs and goals and ensure that your property is well-maintained and profitable.

With our full property management services, you can enjoy the benefits of owning a property without any of the hassle.
