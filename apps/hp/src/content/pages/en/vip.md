## What Exclusive access gives you

- **The most discreet listings.** Properties whose owners share them only with Exclusive members — on top of the secret portfolio that every member sees.
- **The exact location and address** of each property, not only the village.
- **The full picture:** price history, hidden prices, videos, documents and floor plans.
- **Priority** on newly listed properties — you are informed before the market.
- **A dedicated agent** with priority at every level of communication.
- **Discretion.** Your personal details and requests stay confidential; no other agent or company can see them.

## How it works

1. **Register for free** as a buyer, if you are not a member yet.
2. **Ask for Exclusive access** with the form below — tell us briefly what you are looking for.
3. **Our office grants it** after a short conversation with an adviser, and you receive notifications for new offers and price drops before anyone else.

Your time is the most valuable thing and we respect it: our agents help you find what you want and guide you through the process without wasted time.
