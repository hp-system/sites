## Three levels of access

Every visitor sees our properties with their municipality, price, description and the photos we choose to show. A **free member** also sees the village or area, the map at village level and the secret portfolio, and can keep favourites and saved searches. **Exclusive access** is granted by our office on request and opens everything else: the exact location, the price history, videos and documents, and the most discreet listings. Your role — buyer, owner or agent — is separate from your level of access: an owner can be an Exclusive member too.
