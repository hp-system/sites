## What a free member gets

- **The village or area of every property.** Visitors see only the municipality; members see where in it the property is.
- **The map at village level**, so you can judge the distance to the beach, the shops and the roads before a viewing.
- **The secret portfolio** — properties whose owners prefer not to advertise them publicly.
- **Favourites**, to keep the properties you like in one place and compare them.
- **Saved searches**, so you can come back to your criteria any time.

## How to register

1. Choose **Register** and enter your name, e-mail and phone.
2. Confirm with the code we send you — no password to remember.
3. Browse with full member access. If you want more, ask for [Exclusive access](/en/vip).

You can also simply [send us your request](/en/contact) — budget, area and type are enough — and an adviser replies with a shortlist.
