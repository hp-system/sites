## What an owner member gets

- **How your property performs.** See the visits to your property and how they change over time.
- **The interest it receives.** Follow the requests and the interest buyers show, while their personal data stay protected.
- **Your property's readiness.** See which documents make your property ready for a quick sale, and what is still missing.
- **Suggestions from our team** to raise your property's visibility and reach the right buyers.

## How to register

1. List your property with us — [add your property](/en/add-your-property) or [assign it to us](/en/work-with-us-exclusively).
2. Choose **Register** and enter your name, e-mail and phone; we link your account to your property.
3. Sign in any time to follow your property. Our advisers stay your contact for every step.
